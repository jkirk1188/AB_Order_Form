How to Use the Order Guide
Placing an Order
1
Select an account from the dropdown at the top. The order form will populate with that customer's items automatically.
2
Set the Delivery Date by tapping the yellow date field.
3
Enter a quantity in the Qty field for each item you're ordering. Leave blank if not ordering that item.
4
Select the unit — CS, EA, or LB — using the dropdown next to each quantity. Defaults to CS.
5
Add any line notes in the Special Requests column for items that need specific instructions (trim, temp, cut, etc.).
6
Use the Special Order Requests box at the bottom for any off-list items or general order notes.
The Total Line Items counter at the bottom updates automatically as you fill in quantities.
Sending the Order
1
Tap Export to PDF at the bottom of the form.
2
Safari's print preview will open. Pinch outward on the preview to convert it to a PDF file.
3
Tap the Share button and save the PDF to your Files app.
4
Open Outlook, compose a new email to CS, and attach the PDF from your Files app.
Company Outlook protections prevent direct browser-to-email attachment. Saving to Files first then attaching in Outlook is the reliable workaround.
Adding to Your Home Screen
1
Open the app URL in Safari (not Chrome).
2
Tap the Share button (box with arrow) at the bottom of Safari.
3
Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. Name it "AB Orders" and tap Add.
Once added to your home screen, the app opens full screen like a native app with no browser bars.
Tips
✓
You can switch accounts using the dropdown at the top without logging out.
✓
Switching accounts clears the form — export or screenshot first if needed.
✓
Your PIN is specific to your rep number. Contact your manager if you need it reset.
